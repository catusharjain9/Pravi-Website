# PRAVI Insights — content review policy

## Before publishing a technical article
1. Define the audience, question and practical action the reader should be able to take.
2. Prefer current primary sources for statutory facts: the Income Tax Department, GST Portal, Ministry of Corporate Affairs and the relevant regulator/authority.
3. Record the applicable tax/financial year, taxpayer/entity assumptions and source links where relevant. Do not generalise one entity's position to every reader.
4. Distinguish official requirements, practical recommendations and illustrative examples. Label fictional examples clearly.
5. Verify all rates, thresholds, forms, dates and section references for the relevant period before publishing. For material legal/tax conclusions, obtain appropriate professional review.
6. Enter an author/reviewer only if the person and details are verified and authorised for publication. Enter a review date only when a substantive review actually occurred.
7. Avoid guarantees, unsupported outcome claims, fake client cases, invented statistics and automated mass-produced articles.
8. Check that any named client or ecosystem relationship is authorised for public identification.
9. Test the rendered article, links, mobile layout, metadata and whether draft content is inaccessible to anonymous visitors.
10. Re-review when a law, official portal process, form or deadline changes. Do not change dates just to make unchanged content appear fresh.

## Primary reference starting points
- Income Tax Department: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/Pages/tax-services.aspx
- Income Tax e-Filing portal: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/
- GST Portal: https://www.gst.gov.in/
- Ministry of Corporate Affairs: https://www.mca.gov.in/
- Google Search Central people-first content guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/

These references do not certify the website or replace review of the law and facts applicable to a specific engagement.
