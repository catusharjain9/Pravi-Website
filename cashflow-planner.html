CASH PLANNING WORKPAPER

13-week cash flow planner

A finance-team cash visibility tool. Map expected receipts and payments by week, identify potential cash shortfalls early and export a forecast workpaper for review.

← Back to business tools

Forecast assumptions

Enter cash at the start of week 1, a minimum cash buffer and weekly receipts/payments. The workpaper rolls the closing balance into the next week and runs a downside stress case.

Use expected cash collection/payment dates—not invoice accounting dates. Include GST and other statutory payments on the week cash is expected to leave the bank. Do not double-count amounts already included in opening cash or forecasts.

Weekly cash forecast

Enter figures in ₹. “Customer collections” and “Other inflows” are positive cash inflows. Expense fields are positive cash outflows; opening, net flow and closing cash calculate automatically.
