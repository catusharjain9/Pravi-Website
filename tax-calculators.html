PRACTICAL TAX WORKPAPERS

Tax calculators and validation tools

Calculate common GST and TDS scenarios, model ITC utilisation, and run format checks. Each tool exposes its assumptions so the output can be reviewed rather than treated as an automatic tax opinion.

← Back to business tools

GST amount calculator

Calculate GST inclusive/exclusive amounts and split tax by intra-state or inter-state supply.

GST input tax credit set-off model

Allocate available credit against output liability by tax head. This is a deterministic planning model; confirm current statutory utilisation order, restrictions and portal treatment before filing.

GST interest and late-fee estimator

Enter the dates and applicable rate/cap from the return type and current notification. The estimator deliberately keeps rates and caps editable because late-fee relief and return-specific rules can change.

TDS interest and late-filing fee estimator

Month-or-part interest periods are counted by calendar month boundaries. Choose the event type and confirm the applicable statutory section, transaction facts, and current law before use.

Income-tax block depreciation estimator (WDV)

Estimate a block-level depreciation figure using an editable rate, opening WDV, additions split by the 180-day condition and disposal proceeds. The rate is not selected automatically; use the rate and treatment applicable to the asset block and assessment year.

This does not automatically identify asset blocks, special rates, additional depreciation, passenger-car or energy-device exceptions, business-use percentage, succession, block cessation, section 50 gains, or other special rules. It is not a statutory computation without case-specific review.

GSTIN and HSN format checks

These are format checks only. They do not query GSTN, confirm registration status, validate a live taxpayer record, or identify an applicable HSN tax rate.

For live checks, use the official GST taxpayer search. For rate references, consult the CBIC GST rates page; confirm the relevant notification and effective date.

Important: These are editable-input planning workpapers, not legal or filing determinations. Confirm current rates, caps, exemptions, return type, tax period, effective dates, special notifications, credit restrictions and facts. The GSTIN/HSN checker is not a live lookup. Outputs should be reviewed and retained with source records.
