How this area works

A tax assignment starts with the issue and the applicable deadline.

The work may be a routine return, a TDS reconciliation, a response to an information request or a disputed issue. Identifying the proceeding, relevant period, records and due date helps determine the appropriate next step.

Potential scope

What this support can include.

Return, TDS and issue-analysis tasks are selected according to the matter and the accepted scope.

Return preparation support

Organise income, expense, deduction and supporting schedules needed for preparation and review.

TDS compliance support

Map applicable information, reconcile records and prepare the agreed periodic compliance work.

Tax issue analysis

Identify the specific question, applicable facts, relevant documents and points that require research.

Assessment documentation

Help structure factual responses, reconciliations and supporting evidence, subject to the proceeding and authority to act.

Applications and registrations

Support documentation for relevant tax onboarding or applications, where applicable and accepted.

Deadline and document map

Set out the immediate due dates, missing inputs, responsible people and review steps.

Before the first discussion

Information that helps define the work.

A broad outline is sufficient for an initial enquiry. Do not send sensitive IDs, passwords, bank credentials or confidential client records in the initial WhatsApp message.

PREPARATION CHECKLIST

  • Relevant notices or department communications and their complete dates
  • The relevant return, computation and prior submissions if applicable
  • Books, ledgers, TDS statements / certificates and reconciliation schedules
  • A short summary of the question, requested outcome and due date

Use this list to prepare for the first discussion; the actual records needed depend on the tax matter and proceeding.

Frequently asked questions

Before you proceed.

Can you review a tax notice?

A preliminary conversation can identify the type of notice, date, requested information and urgency. Acceptance of a response or representation assignment depends on facts, scope and applicable professional eligibility.

Can I send the full notice on WhatsApp?

Please do not send tax identifiers or sensitive records in an initial message. Share the broad issue and deadline first; an appropriate secure exchange route can be agreed if an engagement proceeds.

Discuss the requirement

Clarify the tax question and its deadline.

Share the type of tax matter, relevant period and any response date. Keep identifiers and confidential records out of the initial enquiry.
