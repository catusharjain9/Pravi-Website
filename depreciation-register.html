Whole-life depreciation schedule

Year-by-year schedule from the financial year in which the asset is put to use through the end of its useful life. This workpaper uses straight-line depreciation, actual days in each financial year, and the dates/life/residual values you entered.

AssetFinancial yearOpening carrying amountDays in useDepreciationAccumulated depreciationClosing carrying amount

The final period is adjusted to the remaining depreciable amount so the full-life schedule does not exceed cost less residual value. The schedule is a planning estimate and does not automatically apply statutory change-in-estimate, disposal, impairment, component, tax block or 180-day rules.

Scope: This is a configurable planning workpaper, not an automatic Companies Act Schedule II or Income-tax Act depreciation engine. It does not classify assets, determine statutory rates, apply component accounting, additions/disposals, impairments, change-in-estimate rules, block-of-assets rules or the income-tax 180-day restriction. Review the calculation and assumptions before using it in statutory financial statements or tax returns.
