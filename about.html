About PRAVI

Practical finance support for people building a business.

PRAVI BUSINESS ADVISORS LLP works across startup foundations, accounting, tax and compliance, management reporting and finance planning. The aim is to help founders understand the work in front of them, define a realistic scope and build routines that can grow with the business.

Our point of view

Start with the founder’s real question.

A founder may need to choose an entity structure, organise financial records, understand an obligation or decide how long cash will last. The useful answer starts with the business model, stage, available evidence and the decision that needs to be made.

The aim is not a one-size-fits-all package. It is to understand the founder’s context, define the work and make the next step clearer.

PRAVI’s service model is designed to connect recurring compliance with dependable accounting records and useful management information. Depending on the engagement, support may also include due-diligence readiness, budget and feasibility work, startup documentation coordination, and virtual CFO-style finance support.

Each organisation has its own systems, deadlines, transaction history and priorities. The first step is to understand the question, review the available information, identify what is missing and agree a realistic scope, required inputs, timelines and responsibility split.

Where an assignment calls for statutory audit, assurance, certification, legal drafting, tax representation or other reserved work, it must be accepted only after the relevant professional eligibility, authorisation, independence and appointment requirements have been confirmed.

Accountability before work begins

Make the people, outputs and boundaries explicit.

For an accepted engagement, these are the points to clarify in writing—not assumptions to leave unresolved.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Named points of contact

Identify who provides information, who reviews it, who approves decisions and how questions or escalations will be handled.

02 / DELIVERABLES

Defined work products

Agree the reports, schedules, returns, documentation or analysis expected, together with inputs, milestones and exclusions.

03 / PROFESSIONAL FIT

Eligibility confirmed first

Where a task is reserved or regulated, confirm qualifications, appointment, independence and authorisation before accepting it.
