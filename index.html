Good advisory work should make the task, the information needed and the next action easier to understand.

    Understand

  2. 02

    Scope

  3. 03

    Onboard

    Confirm the document list, access arrangements, accountable contacts and review timetable.

  4. 04

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

A few practical points about how work is scoped and how an initial conversation can begin.

What should I share in the first enquiry?

A short description of the entity, service area, broad timing and what you are trying to resolve is enough to begin. Avoid sharing passwords, bank credentials, full tax identifiers or sensitive records over an initial WhatsApp message.

Can I ask about more than one service?

Yes. Accounting, tax, compliance and planning questions can overlap. Describe the full situation and the scope can be discussed before any work is agreed.

Will submitting the website form send an email?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser. You review it and choose whether to press Send in WhatsApp. The website itself does not deliver or store the enquiry.

Can PRAVI undertake audits or tax representation?

These assignments have specific legal, professional, appointment and independence requirements. The scope will be confirmed only after the facts and eligibility requirements are reviewed; the website does not promise that every assignment is available.
