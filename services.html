01 / ASSURANCE

Audit & assurance

Review and assurance-related work may include audit planning support, internal audit, risk review, inventory and fixed-asset verification, expenditure review and financial or legal due-diligence support. The precise nature of the assignment matters: independent audit opinions, statutory appointments and attest work carry specific requirements.

  • Internal audit planning and process review
  • Risk and control observations
  • Inventory and fixed-asset verification support
  • Expense and transaction review, where scoped
  • Financial and legal due-diligence coordination
  • Statutory audit or assurance only where the required eligibility, appointment, independence and signing authority are satisfied
02 / DIRECT TAX

Direct tax

View the direct-tax service page and preparation guide ↗

Support to organise income-tax information, prepare recurring compliance work and evaluate tax questions against the applicable facts and law. Assessment, appellate and representation matters need separate review of the proceeding, deadlines, authorisation and professional eligibility.

  • Income-tax return preparation support
  • TDS return and related compliance support
  • Tax research, advisory notes and issue analysis
  • Assessment response and appellate documentation support
  • Lower or nil deduction certificate application support, as applicable
  • NGO / charitable registration and renewal documentation involving sections 12AB and 80G, subject to eligibility and approval by authorities
  • Entity registration and tax onboarding coordination
03 / INDIRECT TAX

Indirect tax & GST

GST support focuses on registrations, return preparation, reconciliation of accounting records with available GST portal data, advisory questions and refund documentation. Audit/attestation or representation work is separately scoped and handled only where permitted and appropriately authorised.

  • GST registration and amendment support
  • GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and related return preparation support
  • Matching books with GSTR-2A / GSTR-2B and identifying differences
  • Input tax credit and outward-supply reconciliation support
  • GST advisory and refund documentation
  • Audit and proceeding support where applicable and eligible
04 / RECORDS

Bookkeeping, accounting & MIS

Accounting support can be planned around the entity’s transaction volume, current records and reporting needs. Remote workflows may use Tally, Zoho or another agreed accounting system. Reports depend on the completeness and accuracy of information supplied and the reporting scope agreed.

  • Bookkeeping and transaction classification
  • Bank, ledger and control-account reconciliations
  • Monthly income statement and management reports
  • Management Information System (MIS) design and reporting pack
  • Period-end close and financial schedule preparation support
  • Accounting manual design, implementation and review
  • Document organisation and due-diligence readiness
05 / STARTUP

Startup, LLP & company support

Support for setting up a business and creating an orderly compliance foundation, from entity-structure considerations to ongoing records and transaction documentation. Legal drafting, regulatory filings, transaction advice and reserved activities are handled within applicable eligibility and authorisation boundaries.

  • Company and LLP formation pathway and documentation support
  • Entity registration, tax onboarding and recurring compliance calendars
  • Applicability mapping for licences and registrations
  • Board / partner meeting records and resolution documentation
  • Founder and shareholder agreements, MOUs and NDAs: drafting coordination, subject to appropriate legal review
  • Transaction-related documentation support for matters such as rights issues, secondary transfers, debentures, preference capital or bonus issues, subject to specific legal and regulatory advice
  • Startup compliance checklists and responsibility mapping
  • ROC compliance coordination, including annual/event-based filing requirements and corporate records, subject to applicable scope
  • FEMA applicability review and coordination for relevant foreign investment, remittance or cross-border transactions, with specialist input where required
  • MSME-related compliance and documentation support, including applicability checks for relevant payment and registration requirements
06 / ADVISORY

Business advisory, planning & feasibility

Structured business planning connects the commercial question to the available numbers, resources and operating constraints. Work may include building a plan, evaluating an investment or project, or documenting processes and performance measures.

  • Business plans and business-policy frameworks
  • Project feasibility study coordination and financial modelling support
  • Budgeting, forecasting and periodic follow-up
  • Working-capital cycle and cash-conversion analysis
  • Key financial ratio and performance review
  • Growth, market-entry and operating-model decision support
  • Accounting procedures and internal manual design
07 / FINANCE

Virtual CFO & finance-function support

For small and growing businesses, a virtual CFO-style engagement can complement an internal accounts person or existing finance team with structured reporting, forecasts and decision materials. The exact cadence and responsibility split are agreed with the client; this description is not a promise of continuous or 24/7 availability.

  • Periodic financial review and management meeting pack
  • Cash-flow forecasts and working-capital visibility
  • Annual budgets, rolling forecasts and scenario analysis
  • Monthly MIS, KPI dashboard and budget-versus-actual review
  • Finance calendar, close process and reporting responsibilities
  • Decision support for expansion, investment and resource allocation
  • Coordination between business owners, accounts staff and other advisers
08 / COORDINATION

Compliance coordination & other support

Some requirements cut across tax, accounts, company law and operations. Where the correct route is not initially clear, the first step is to map the question, identify the missing records and determine what work or specialist input may be needed.

  • Compliance calendars and responsibility matrices
  • Document and information request lists
  • Open-item registers and deadline tracking
  • Process walkthroughs and workflow mapping
  • Coordination with other qualified or authorised professionals as needed
Professional eligibility and scope: The availability of statutory audits, attestations, certifications, legal opinions, tax representation and other reserved work depends on applicable law, professional qualifications, appointment, independence and authorisation requirements. Every engagement is subject to review and a separately agreed scope. No service description is a guarantee that a particular assignment can be accepted.
