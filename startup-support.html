Service layer 04 · Startup & entity support

Founder-first setup and compliance for your startup.

Turn early decisions into an organised foundation: clarify the entity pathway, set up core finance records, identify applicable registrations and assign ownership for recurring tasks.

Discuss this service ↗Request a call

Registration and compliance applicability depends on the entity’s facts and current requirements; there is no universal setup path for every startup.

How this area works

Turn founder decisions into a workable operating foundation.

Startup requirements vary with legal form, ownership, turnover, transactions, employees, funding and operating model. The practical first step is to understand these facts, identify which registrations and records may apply, and assign responsibility for recurring tasks.

Potential scope

What this support can include.

Applicable tasks must be confirmed against the entity, activity, ownership and current requirements.

Entity setup pathway

Compare the information and decision points needed before selecting an incorporation or registration route; not a substitute for legal advice.

Registration and onboarding map

Map the registrations and documents likely to be relevant for the selected structure and activity.

Recurring entity compliance

Set out a recurring obligation calendar, information owners and documentation workflow.

Corporate records

Support organisation of notices, minutes, resolutions, registers and other records within the agreed scope.

Founder and transaction documents

Coordinate required inputs and specialist review for founder arrangements, MOUs, NDAs or transaction-specific documentation.

Accounting and finance foundation

Set up a basic record flow, month-end responsibility map and management reporting routine.

ROC compliance

Map relevant company/LLP filing requirements, event-based obligations, corporate records and filing dependencies.

FEMA coordination

Identify potential FEMA questions around foreign investment, remittances or cross-border transactions and coordinate specialist review where required.

MSME compliance support

Review relevant MSME registration and payment-related compliance questions based on the entity, supplier/customer facts and applicable rules.

USEFUL CONTEXT

  • Broad business activity and proposed entity type, if decided
  • Proposed owners/partners and intended start date
  • Location and regulated activity questions needing confirmation
  • Expected employees, turnover/transactions or tax registrations if relevant
