How recurring items work

When you mark a recurring item as filed/completed, the tracker keeps the completed entry for history and creates a new open entry using the next monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or annual date. Review the next period label and due date before relying on it.

The next due date is a mechanical recurrence based on the date you entered. It does not account for weekends, public holidays, extensions, staggered categories or notifications.

Local-only record keeping.
Register records are stored in this browser on this device. They are not synced with staff, clients, PRAVI or cloud storage. Export the full JSON backup routinely. Avoid passwords, PAN/Aadhaar, bank credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal data in the notes.
This is a configurable task-management aid, not a legal compliance opinion, authoritative statutory calendar or filing integration. The user is responsible for confirming applicability, due date, filing period and supporting evidence. Browser storage may be cleared by device/browser settings; keep backups.
