Before you rely on an output

Designed for reviewable work, not automatic filing

These tools are starting points for your working papers. Use the source documents, current rules and reviewer judgement to confirm the result.

01

Keep the source data

Retain the original files, note any mapping changes and export the workpaper or backup before closing the tab.

02

Check assumptions and exceptions

Investigate duplicates, unmatched rows, missing periods, opening balances and unusual movements instead of accepting a summary total alone.

03

Confirm rules independently

Tax eligibility, statutory due dates, depreciation treatment and accounting classifications depend on facts and current law. The tools do not file returns or replace professional review.
