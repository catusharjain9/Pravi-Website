Virtual CFO support works best with a clear responsibility split.

The term “virtual CFO” can mean different things from one provider to another. Before work starts, define the decisions to support, recurring outputs, management access, team responsibilities and what the engagement excludes.

1. Separate bookkeeping from finance oversight

Bookkeeping records and classifies transactions. Finance oversight may use those records to review trends, forecast cash, compare actuals with budget and prepare decision materials. These functions interact but are not identical, and each needs a clear owner.

2. Define the recurring outputs

Agree whether the engagement includes monthly MIS, cash-flow forecast, budget-versus-actual analysis, working-capital review, management meeting or an action log. Define the reporting basis and how incomplete data will be marked.

3. Confirm the cadence and availability

State the reporting cut-off, when data must arrive, when the draft will be reviewed, meeting frequency and what counts as urgent. Do not assume continuous or 24/7 availability unless specifically contracted.

4. Clarify responsibilities and approvals

Map the work split among the business owner, internal accounts staff, provider and other authorised professionals. Identify who prepares, reviews, approves, signs and files each item.

5. Understand forecast limitations

Forecasts depend on assumptions about receipts, payments, sales, costs, borrowing and operating conditions. Scenarios should show key sensitivities and should not be presented as guaranteed outcomes.

6. Review whether the model is helping

At an agreed interval, check if the reporting arrives in time, unresolved items are closing, assumptions remain relevant and management decisions are supported by the available information.

Important: This guide is general preparation material, not a legal opinion, tax opinion, audit, assurance, certification or confirmation that a specific statutory requirement applies. Verify the current requirements for the facts and period in question.

At a glance

Practical takeaways

  • Ask for the exact deliverables and frequency rather than relying on a label.
  • Check whether the provider will manage tasks, advise on them, or both.
  • Agree scope, fee, approvals, response times and professional boundaries in writing.
  • Virtual CFO support does not automatically replace statutory appointments or internal accountability.
