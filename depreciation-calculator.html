Depreciation & deferred tax calculator

Calculate Companies Act book depreciation and Income-tax block depreciation independently, compare carrying amount with tax written-down value, and prepare a year-wise deferred tax schedule.

← Back to business tools

1. Computation settings

Select the reporting financial year and projection period. Tax-law framework is mapped to the selected year; verify the applicable provisions and rates before filing or signing financial statements.

FY 2026–27: Income-tax Act, 2025 and Income-tax Rules, 2026 framework applies from 1 April 2026. Confirm current Appendix I rates and any special conditions.

2. Companies Act – asset register

Add individual assets. Schedule II useful life suggestions are provided for common asset classes; you can override the life and residual value where supported by the applicable accounting policy and disclosures.

3. Income-tax – block of assets

Enter opening tax WDV and current-year additions/disposals for each block. The calculator applies the 180-day restriction to additions and projects future years without assumed additions or disposals.

Tax depreciation is calculated on a block basis. Additions must be split by actual days of use in the relevant previous/tax year. Sale proceeds reduce the relevant block. If all assets in a block cease to exist, balancing charge / short-term capital gain or terminal loss treatment needs a separate tax computation.

4. Deferred tax assumptions

DTA recognition is not automatic: apply the recognition threshold under the entity’s applicable framework (AS 22 or Ind AS 12), including the required evidence of future taxable profits and applicable exceptions. This tool computes depreciation-related temporary differences only; it does not include tax losses, provisions, revaluations, grants, leases, business combinations or other temporary differences.

Amounts shown are estimates generated from the inputs and assumptions. The year-wise deferred tax is calculated on the aggregate net temporary difference across the entered book assets and tax blocks. Use this net presentation only where offsetting criteria under the applicable standard are met for the same taxable entity and taxation authority. Separate runs or asset/block-level workings are needed where gross DTA/DTL balances must be separately assessed. The year-wise net deferred tax is the closing DTL less closing DTA. The movement is the change in net deferred tax from the previous reporting date, not automatically the P&L tax charge in all circumstances.

Calculation methodology & review controls

  • Companies Act: depreciable amount is cost less residual value; Schedule II useful life is suggested by asset class. SLM or WDV is calculated asset-wise with date-based pro-rata.
  • Income-tax: depreciation is computed on each block's opening WDV and eligible additions; additions put to use for less than 180 days receive half of the normal rate.
  • Deferred tax: compares book carrying amount with tax base (tax WDV). Positive book NBV less tax WDV generally creates a taxable temporary difference / DTL; the reverse generally creates a deductible temporary difference / DTA.
  • Tax regime restrictions, additional depreciation eligibility, block cessation, special rates, power-generation assets and other exceptions require review.

Reference framework: Companies Act, 2013 Schedule II; Income-tax Appendix I rates; Ind AS 12 where applicable. Tax law framework changes from 1 April 2026. Preset useful lives and tax rates are starting points only; verify classification, effective rules, exceptions and rates for the relevant year.
