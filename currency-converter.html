Rate data provided by exchangerate.dev. The no-key public feed is delayed by 15 minutes; true live observations require a paid API plan and a protected server-side key. Daily reference fallback data is identified separately.

Important: This is an indicative mid-market conversion for reference only, not a bank quote, remittance quote or settlement rate. Banks and payment providers may apply their own spread, fees and taxes. Foreign-exchange markets operate on business days; weekend and holiday observations may be stale or unavailable.

Currency and calculation standard

The converter uses three-letter ISO 4217 currency codes, keeps the direction explicit (1 unit of the source currency equals the displayed target-currency rate), and calculates the converted amount without intermediate rounding. Only the displayed result is formatted to the target currency’s standard minor-unit precision. Crypto-assets, precious metals and obsolete currency codes are excluded.
