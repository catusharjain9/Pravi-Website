Local processing and privacy

Document processing runs in your browser; this page does not send document files to PRAVI servers. The page loads PDF-processing libraries from jsDelivr, so an internet connection is required. Use a trusted, updated device and avoid shared computers for confidential records. Clear downloaded files and browser data when appropriate. See the third-party library notices.

Output quality: Compress PDF converts pages to images. Text may no longer be searchable or selectable, fine print may lose clarity, and the result may be larger than the original. Keep your original file.
