How this area works

Agree the reporting rhythm and the decisions it should support.

A virtual CFO-style arrangement should define the information available, reporting cadence, management meeting format and division of responsibility between the business, accounts team and adviser. It is not a substitute for clear source records or an agreed scope of authority.

Potential scope

How we can help.

The reporting cadence and decision support depend on the business, available records and agreed role boundaries.

Periodic performance review

Review selected financial metrics, key movements and unresolved questions with management.

Cash-flow forecasting

Organise opening cash, expected receipts, planned payments, timing assumptions and scenarios.

Budget-versus-actual review

Compare outcomes against an agreed budget and record the reasons requiring follow-up.

MIS and management pack

Define the recurring output, reporting calendar, data sources and review responsibilities.

Working-capital and margin views

Focus analysis on agreed drivers such as receivables, stock, payables and cost categories.

Finance process coordination

Clarify the split among accounts staff, management, external professionals and approvers.
