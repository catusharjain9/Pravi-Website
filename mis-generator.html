Before you circulate a monthly MIS

  • Reconcile the trial balance to the general ledger and bank balances.
  • Close sales, purchase, payroll, accrual and cut-off entries for the month.
  • Review GST/TDS liabilities, receivables ageing, payables ageing and inventory valuation separately.
  • Compare actuals with a consistently classified prior period and approved budget.
  • Document unusual variances and obtain the appropriate reviewer’s sign-off.
Data mapping is a control.
Do not upload a ledger export with unverified headings and assume the report is correct. First standardize the category for every account. A category mistake can materially distort gross margin, EBITDA or working-capital metrics.
