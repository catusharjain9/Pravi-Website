Service layer 02 · Accounting & reporting

Accounting that gives the business a clearer monthly picture.

Organise the books, close the period, reconcile the balances and prepare a reporting pack that explains the current position and the items needing attention.

Discuss this service ↗Request a call

Software access and integrations are not implied; systems and responsibilities are agreed separately.

How this area works

Build the workflow around the records and decisions that matter.

The starting point is the state of the books: transaction classification, bank and ledger reconciliations, period-end cut-off and the questions management needs answered. A defined clean-up and a recurring monthly close may need different scopes.

Potential scope

What this support can include.

The final workflow depends on the books, reporting basis, records available and review responsibilities.

Bookkeeping and classification

Record and organise transactions using the agreed source documents and accounting policies.

Bank and ledger reconciliation

Compare records, investigate outstanding entries and maintain an exception list for review.

Period-end close

Coordinate schedules, cut-off matters and ledger checks required for the agreed close process.

Financial statement preparation support

Prepare agreed financial statement schedules or drafts based on the records and review responsibilities defined.

Monthly MIS pack

Structure revenue, cost, margin, working-capital, cash and open-action views relevant to the business.

Process and system workflows

Document recurring tasks, access responsibilities, approvals and the hand-offs between business and accounts teams.

Frequently asked questions

Before you proceed.

Can you work with Tally or Zoho?

The engagement can be discussed around Tally, Zoho or another agreed accounting environment. This page does not claim an existing integration, software partnership or direct system access.

Will MIS show what decisions to make?

MIS can organise information and highlight trends or questions. Interpretation depends on the reliability of the underlying data, business context and assumptions; it does not guarantee a business outcome.
