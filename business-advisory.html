Forecasts and feasibility work support decisions; they do not guarantee results, funding or commercial success.

How this area works

Define the decision before building the model.

A useful advisory assignment begins with the decision to be made, the alternatives under consideration and the assumptions that could change the answer. Financial models can compare scenarios, but their usefulness depends on the quality of inputs and the limits made explicit.

Potential scope

What this support can include.

The work is tailored to the decision, available data, assumptions and any specialist input required.

Business plan structure

Clarify the model, customer/market assumptions, cost base, resource needs and milestones.

Budgets and forecasts

Build the agreed period view and document assumptions that materially influence results.

Working-capital review

Map receivables, inventory, payables, cash conversion and near-term funding pressure.

Feasibility and scenario analysis

Compare scenarios, key sensitivities, required investment and operational dependencies.

Financial ratios and management review

Prepare a coherent view of selected metrics with definitions and limitations.

Policies, procedures and accounting manuals

Document recurring responsibilities, approvals, workflows and review points.

Frequently asked questions

Before you proceed.

Can the model predict the exact result?

No. Forecasts estimate outcomes under stated assumptions. Actual performance depends on events and decisions that cannot be assured in advance.

Can you help assess a project?

A scoped feasibility assignment can examine the available data, assumptions, scenarios and dependencies. Technical, legal, regulatory or industry-specific opinions may require other qualified professionals.
