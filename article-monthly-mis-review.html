Monthly MIS: what a business owner should review

A decision-focused monthly review of performance, cash, working capital and follow-up actions.

PRAVI BUSINESS ADVISORS LLPPractical guide

A monthly management information system (MIS) is useful when it helps management understand what changed, why it changed and what needs attention. A report pack should be built around decisions, not simply the number of spreadsheets it contains.

1. Revenue and profitability

Review revenue against the prior period, budget or forecast where those comparisons are meaningful. Separate volume, price, product or customer mix effects where the underlying data supports that analysis. Review gross margin and operating expenses, and investigate material movements rather than assuming every variance is an error.

2. Cash and working capital

  • Review available bank balances and expected near-term cash commitments.
  • Identify overdue receivables, disputed invoices and collection owners.
  • Review supplier dues and upcoming statutory or debt payments.
  • Compare forecast receipts and payments with actual outcomes to improve assumptions.

3. Balance-sheet quality

Review bank reconciliations, advances, loans, statutory balances, inventory where relevant, fixed assets and suspense or clearing accounts. A balance that has rolled forward for months without explanation should have an owner, evidence and a target resolution date.

4. Explain the exceptions

Use a short variance log: issue, amount or operational impact, likely explanation, evidence needed, owner and next action. Distinguish confirmed facts from estimates or hypotheses. If source data is incomplete, state the limitation rather than presenting a precise-looking but unreliable conclusion.

5. End with actions

Every review should finish with a small number of prioritised actions, named owners and dates for follow-up. Track whether last month's actions were completed and whether the expected result occurred.

Practical takeaway: a useful MIS connects financial results to cash, working capital, risks and specific management actions.

Suggested monthly review pack

  • Profit and loss summary with meaningful comparisons.
  • Cash position and short-term cash-flow view.
  • Receivables and payables ageing.
  • Key balance-sheet reconciliations and open items.
  • Variance commentary and action tracker.
