Practical guides

Three useful ways to prepare.

General checklists intended to make your own records and questions easier to organise.

Accounting

Before a month-end review

A practical sequence to help make a periodic accounts review more useful.

  • Collect bank and loan statements.
  • Review open receivables and payables.
  • List large or unusual transactions.
  • Identify unreconciled balances.
  • Record questions for follow-up.
Tax & compliance

Before a compliance discussion

Gather context before asking for an assessment of a filing or open issue.

  • Identify the entity and relevant period.
  • Write down the question or notice received.
  • List filings already completed or pending.
  • Organise the related records.
  • Note the response or business deadline.
Planning & MIS

Before building a forecast

Make the business assumptions visible rather than relying on a single headline number.

  • Understand the current cash position.
  • List expected receipts and outflows.
  • Separate committed and discretionary costs.
  • Identify assumptions and uncertainties.
  • Decide how often the view needs updating.

These guides are not exhaustive and do not replace review of the facts, records or requirements applicable to a particular business.
