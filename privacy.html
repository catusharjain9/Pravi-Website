Privacy & information

Privacy notice — pre-publication draft.

This page explains the intended behaviour of the current static website. It must be checked against the final hosting setup and firm practices before launch.

Draft / review required before launch. This text is a plain-language starting point, not a legal opinion or a statement that the website has been independently reviewed for compliance. Confirm the hosting configuration, applicable obligations and actual firm practices before publishing.

1. Who operates this website?

The website identifies PRAVI BUSINESS ADVISORS LLP as the organisation visitors may contact. The official business email, registered or office address and website domain have not yet been supplied for publication.

2. What happens when you use the enquiry form?

The current website is a static front-end. It does not send the form to a website server, email inbox or database, and does not intentionally store the form contents. After the browser validates the required fields, choosing “Continue to WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message. The visitor must review and press Send. No message is delivered by the website itself.

3. Information you choose to include

The form asks for a name, email address, service area and short description, with phone, business name and timing optional. Enter only information necessary for an initial enquiry. Do not include passwords, banking credentials, tax identifiers, payment details or confidential client records.

4. WhatsApp, PDF tools and external services

If you continue to WhatsApp, that service processes information under its own terms, privacy notice and settings. The PDF workspace loads pinned JavaScript libraries from jsDelivr to perform browser-based PDF operations; loading those scripts creates requests to the CDN and may expose technical connection data under the CDN provider’s policies. PDF and image file contents are processed in the visitor’s browser and are not intentionally uploaded to PRAVI servers by the PDF tools. The website does not intentionally load analytics or advertising pixels. Confirm the actual host, CDN terms and deployed network behaviour before publication.

5. Insights publishing dashboard

The package includes a proposed private article-management dashboard. In this ZIP, Firebase configuration is still a placeholder, so the dashboard is not operational. If the firm later configures Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, Google sign-in will be used for the authorised administrator account and article records will be stored in the configured Firestore project. Published article fields are intended to be publicly readable; draft content is intended to be restricted by Firestore security rules. Do not use the dashboard for client files, tax records or other confidential engagement data. Before enabling it, confirm Firebase region/settings, logs, access controls, retention, billing and applicable privacy disclosures, and test the rules with authorised and unauthorised accounts.

6. Hosting and server logs

Even though the enquiry form does not send information to the website, the hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses, request timestamps, browser details or security logs. The actual provider, log configuration, retention period and support contacts must be confirmed after hosting is selected. This page should be updated accordingly.

7. Cookies and analytics

The current static website does not intentionally set tracking cookies or use an analytics platform. Confirm this again if the site, hosting or contact flow is changed before publication.

8. Requests and contact

Once the official business contact details are confirmed, add the appropriate email or postal route for privacy-related queries. Until then, visitors can use the telephone number published on the Contact page, while avoiding sensitive information in an initial message.

9. Browser-based PDF workspace

The PDF workspace uses browser-side libraries delivered by jsDelivr. Files selected for merge, split, compression, conversion, rotation, page numbering, watermarking, removal or extraction are processed in the browser. Outputs are downloaded to the visitor’s device. No cloud save or server-side document processing is provided. Browser memory limits, third-party script delivery and the user’s device/network security remain relevant.

10. Review checklist before publishing

  • Confirm the firm's official name, contact address and suitable privacy contact.
  • Review the actual hosting provider's logs, backups, security and retention settings.
  • Confirm whether any analytics, embedded content, tracking or form-handling services are added.
  • Review the text against applicable Indian data-protection and other legal obligations as they apply to the final website and business.
  • Update the effective date and publish only after the review is completed.

No effective date is stated because this draft has not yet been approved for publication.
